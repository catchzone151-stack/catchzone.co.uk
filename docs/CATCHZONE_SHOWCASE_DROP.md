## Responsive behaviour

Treat desktop, tablet, and mobile as art-directed experiences.

### Desktop

- strongest cinematic composition
- richer spatial layering/motion where useful
- preserve legibility of actual UI screens

### Tablet

- simplify layers where needed
- preserve intended device treatment
- avoid shrinking desktop screenshots until unreadable

### Mobile

- stack cleanly
- use sensible crops, focused detail views, or swipeable media when a wide screen would become microscopic
- do not simply scale a desktop composition down

Verify the completed work at desktop, slimmer desktop, tablet, and phone widths.
